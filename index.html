MPK Mini Studio

Click Connect, then allow MIDI access. Works in Chrome, Edge and Opera.

Pads and sound mapping

Press Learn, then hit any pad or key to bind it. Pick a built-in sound or load your own audio file. Any key without a mapping plays the synth. Tick Looper on one pad: press it to repeat the last drum you hit, and press it again to stop. Piano keys are never looped.

Keys

Piano roll

Click an empty cell to add a note, drag a note to move it, drag its right edge to resize it, and double-click to delete. Drag the bars in the velocity lane to change how hard a note plays. With Record on, the keys and pads you play land in the roll, and drum pads appear on their own note rows.

Pad lights (experimental)

This might not work. Akai doesn’t publish a way to change the MPK Mini MK3 pad colors from a web page, so this sends ordinary note messages to the pads and hopes the lights respond. It never sends settings or presets. Move the slider and press Light all pads to see what your controller does.
